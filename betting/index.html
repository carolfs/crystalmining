  2. The year is 2452. You’ve just been hired by Cosmic Crystal Corp (C3) as their new Eco-Credit Analyst after your predecessor abruptly left for a rival company.

  3. With your background in environmental assessment and predictive analytics, you’ll fill a crucial role in C3’s financial department. Your job: forecast the Eco-Credits that C3’s mining operations will receive each day.

  4. On planet Zyxlon, C3 extracts valuable healing crystals daily and sell them on the Intergalactic Market for profit.

  5. The Intergalactic Environmental Protection Agency (IEPA) monitors this activity through their Stellar Sustainability Initiative, which awards or penalizes mining operations with “Eco-Credits”.

  6. Each miner returns from planet Zyxlon with a “crystal score” — the total points from their day’s crystal collection.

  7. The IEPA’s Eco-Credit Auditor then reviews these scores and assigns Eco-Credits accordingly.

  8. Lower crystal scores typically earn higher Eco-Credits because they indicate environmentally responsible mining. Higher scores often result in fewer Eco-Credits or even penalties, impacting C3’s profits.

  9. The Auditor is known for unpredictability. Their calculation method remains confidential, and they introduce variations in their decisions. Similar crystal scores might receive different Eco-Credits.

  10. However, the general principle still applies: higher crystal scores tend to result in fewer Eco-Credits, while lower crystal scores usually lead to more Eco-Credits.

  11. When a miner gets a very high crystal score, the Auditor may give them a negative number of Eco-Credits. This is because high crystal scores can cause significant environmental damage, and the Eco-Credit system is designed to discourage such practices.

  12. The Auditor, for all their eccentricities, is completely impartial when it comes to which miner is receiving the Eco-Credits. In the Eco-Credit calculation system, a miner’s identity is kept anonymous, and the Auditor has no way of knowing whose points they are calculating.

  13. At your workstation, you’ll analyze mining data and crystal score reports. Before miners return each day, you’ll predict their Eco-Credits based on their reported crystal scores.

  14. Your predictions help C3 optimize collection strategies and manage financial projections. Accuracy in your forecasts directly affects company planning and your own performance evaluation.

  15. The more accurate your predictions are, the more points you’ll earn, up to 10 points per prediction.

  16. Study the patterns of crystal collections and Eco-Credits, and use this knowledge to refine your prediction strategy. As C3’s new Eco-Credit Analyst, your forecasting expertise will prove invaluable to the company’s success in the competitive interplanetary crystal market.
